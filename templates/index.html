    Sensor stack on the back

    4 × green LED
    525 nm reflective PPG. The same signal your camera scan reads, but continuous and without a finger on the lens.
    Red + IR LED
    660 / 940 nm pair for SpO₂ during sleep and after alerts.
    2 × photodiode
    Picks up the reflected light. Signal quality (SQI) gates every reading before it counts.
    3-axis accel
    Tells a workout from a resting spike, so 146 BPM on a run stays quiet and 197 BPM at a desk does not.
    Haptic + BLE 5.3
    Buzzes on alerts and syncs to the phone app, which forwards to the PulseGuard server.
